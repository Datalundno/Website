DataLund Gantt chart

Tasks on a timeline with progress, groups, colors, and a today line. Works in Power BI Desktop as a .pbiviz file.

  • Expand / Collapse groups from the toolbar
  • Density presets (Compact / Comfortable / Large) — Format → General
  • Optional time window from today (Format → General)
  • Click a bar to filter the rest of the report
