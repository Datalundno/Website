Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Publisher: Datalund · datalund.no

Datalund visuals (including DataLund Gantt) run in the Power BI sandbox. They do not collect, store, send, or sell personal data.

What the visual uses

  • Only the fields you bind in the report (tasks, dates, progress, groups, and so on).
  • Everything renders locally in Power BI Desktop or the service.
  • No outbound network calls.
  • No cookies, ads, analytics, or third-party trackers.

Permissions

DataLund Gantt asks for no network or storage access, so it cannot reach the internet from the visual.

This website

datalund.no is our product and support site. Email is only used to reply. We do not sell contact info. Hosting logs (IP, user agent, time), if any, are for security and uptime.
