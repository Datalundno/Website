Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Controller: Datalund · datalund.no

Datalund visuals (including DataLund Gantt chart) run in the Power BI sandbox. They do not collect, store, send, or sell personal data. The website may use optional, privacy-friendly analytics only after you consent.

Who we are

Datalund publishes free Power BI visuals and operates datalund.no. Contact: support@datalund.no. This notice covers both the visuals and the website, under the EU GDPR and Norwegian personal data law (including the Personal Data Act).

What the visual uses

  • Only the fields you bind in the report (tasks, dates, progress, groups, and so on).
  • Everything renders locally in Power BI Desktop or the service.
  • No outbound network calls.
  • No cookies, ads, analytics, or third-party trackers inside the visual.

Permissions

DataLund Gantt chart asks for no network or storage access, so it cannot reach the internet from the visual.

This website — analytics

We want to know how many people visit datalund.no and how often the Gantt visual (and sample file) is downloaded. Google Analytics loads only if you accept the banner (opt-in). You can change your mind anytime via Analytics preferences.

  • Tool: Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js), loaded only after consent.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • What we measure: page views and download events (file name), plus standard GA technical data (e.g. approximate location/device derived by Google).
  • What we do not enable: Google ads personalisation, Google signals remarketing, or sale of personal data. Ad storage remains denied in Consent Mode.
  • Cookies: after Accept, Google may set analytics cookies (e.g. _ga). Decline or withdraw consent and we set analytics storage to denied.
  • Consent storage: your Accept/Decline choice is stored in localStorage on your device so we remember it. That storage is used only for consent.
  • Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track: if your browser sends these signals, we treat that as Decline and do not load Google Analytics.
  • Processor / transfers: Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC. Data may be processed in the United States under Google’s terms and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable. See Google’s privacy policy and Google Ads Data Processing Terms.
  • Retention: as configured in our GA4 data-retention settings (Google default unless we shorten it).

If you decline, the site works the same; we simply do not load Google Analytics or count your visit/downloads.

Hosting and email

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical logs (such as IP address, user agent, and time) for security and uptime under legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Email to support@datalund.no is used only to reply to you. We do not sell contact info.

Your rights

Under GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier lawful processing). You may also lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or your local EU/EEA supervisory authority.

Use Analytics preferences to withdraw website analytics consent on this device. For Google’s own data, you can also use Google’s tools described in their privacy policy.
